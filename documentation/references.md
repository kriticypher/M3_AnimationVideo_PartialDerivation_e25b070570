# Academic References & AI Disclosure

## Academic References & Pedagogical Literature

1. **Stewart, James** (2015). *Calculus: Early Transcendentals* (8th Edition). Cengage Learning.
   - Chapter 14: Partial Derivatives (Sections 14.1 Functions of Several Variables, 14.3 Partial Derivatives, 14.4 Tangent Planes and Linear Approximations).
   - Serves as the primary reference for formal difference quotient limits and 3D surface traces.

2. **Thomas, George B., Weir, Maurice D., & Hass, Joel** (2014). *Thomas' Calculus* (13th Edition). Pearson Education.
   - Chapter 14: Partial Derivatives (Directional derivatives, gradient vectors, and geometric tangent plane definitions).

3. **Strang, Gilbert** (1991). *Calculus*. Wellesley-Cambridge Press (MIT OpenCourseWare).
   - Chapter 13: Partial Derivatives and the Gradient. Intuitive matrix interpretations and physical analogies.

4. **Marsden, Jerrold E., & Tromba, Anthony** (2012). *Vector Calculus* (6th Edition). W. H. Freeman.
   - Rigorous foundations for multi-dimensional differential calculus and surface normals.

5. **Sanderson, Grant (3Blue1Brown)**. *Essence of Calculus: Visualizing Multivariable Functions*.
   - Inspiration for 3D slicing planes, dynamic secant line convergence, and pedagogical color theory.
